- Greedy/DAI decision identity has been audited in all 180 protected benchmark cells (540,000 paired decision indices). `python3 scripts/rev1/summarize_greedy_decoupled.py` validates each audit row's trace hash and acceptance against the refreshed benchmark and writes `greedy_decoupled_summary.csv`. In NSFNET E/B/C and GEANT2 B/C, placement matches in all 60,000 decisions per cell, while complete action-pair matches range from 3.49% to 7.19%; all 20 seed-level acceptance values match per cell. The actions are aligned by step on separate trajectories, not evaluated counterfactually on one state.
- The retained serialized SAF-H synthetic-grid timing archive is now in the bundle. `python3 scripts/rev1/summarize_archived_grid_timing.py` exports all 30 seed rows and source hashes. At 100 nodes, feasible-set P95 is 68.441–72.238 ms across the ten seeds, versus P50 of 5.510–5.563 ms. The tail is within each seed's feasible-set construction; the saved percentiles do not identify its finer cause. Historical HARP/SAF-H serialized timing is retained separately and is not attributed to the refreshed N² checkpoint.
- The 60-cell TTL/mask intervention is complete. `python3 scripts/rev1/summarize_mask_expiry_intervention.py` validates both modes, all seed trace hashes, and the current-policy rows against the refreshed benchmark. Post-expiry-mask minus current-policy safe acceptance is +0.065 pp (Vietnam), -0.005 pp (NSFNET), and +0.085 pp (GEANT2); all three bootstrap CIs include zero and Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon p-values exceed 0.78. The intervention retains the pre-expiry observation, so it is not a full runtime order change or a proof that a fix improves acceptance.
